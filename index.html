Our Herd

Full-blood genetics, bred for the long term

Wingfield Wagyu is a full-blood breeding operation built on imported Japanese Black bloodlines, selected over three decades for marbling, structure, and temperament.

Every animal in our herd is registered with the Australian Wagyu Association and pedigree-traced back to its founding genetics.

A long-term approach to breeding, built around proven genetics and careful selection.
