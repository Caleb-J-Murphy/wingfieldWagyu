Our Herd

Full-blood genetics, bred for the long term

Wingfield Wagyu is a full-blood breeding operation built on imported Japanese Black bloodlines, selected over three decades for marbling, structure, and temperament. Every animal in our herd is registered with the Australian Wagyu Association and pedigree-traced back to its founding genetics.

The Land

Raised on open pasture

Our cattle are pasture-raised across open grazing country, with low-stress handling and careful nutrition at every stage. It's a slower way to farm, and it shows in the quality of the animal we produce.
